Lazy Arcade - Provably Fair Verifier

Re-derive any spin yourself. Nothing here talks to our servers; the page computes everything locally in your browser.

How it works

  1. Before your first spin we publish SHA256(serverSeed) on Base. We cannot change the seed after that without the hash changing.
  2. Each spin mixes that secret seed with your client seed and a nonce that increases every spin:
    HMAC_SHA256(serverSeed, "clientSeed:nonce:gameId")
  3. Reel stops come from those bytes by rejection sampling — four bytes at a time, discarding values that would bias the result. Never modulo.
  4. When the seed rotates we reveal it. Paste it below and confirm every spin it ever produced.

Published strip lengths

A reel stop is an index into that reel's virtual strip. These lengths are part of the published paytable.
