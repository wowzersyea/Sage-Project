# B1 — Problem representation in one sentence

Decide whether the PGY-1 gave a problem representation: **one summarising sentence** carrying semantic qualifiers.

## What counts
- A single sentence containing age, relevant epidemiology or background, tempo, and the key features. For example: "Three-year-old, previously healthy, two days of fever and refusal to bear weight, holding the hip flexed and externally rotated."
- Slight compression or two short clauses read as one sentence is fine.

## What does not count
- A list of findings read out in sequence. Recitation is not summary.
- A sentence with no tempo and no qualifiers: "a kid with a sore leg".
- A differential offered instead of a representation.

## Verdict
`true` if a summarising sentence with qualifiers was given. `false` if what was offered was a list of findings.
