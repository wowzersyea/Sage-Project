# B3 — The can't-miss, named as such

Decide whether the PGY-1 explicitly labelled one diagnosis as the can't-miss.

## What counts
- An explicit label: "my can't-miss is…", "the must-not-miss here is…", "the one I cannot afford to be wrong about is…", "worst case is…".
- The label must attach to a specific named diagnosis.

## What does not count
- Listing a dangerous diagnosis without labelling it. Septic arthritis appearing in the list is not the same as it being named as the can't-miss.
- A general statement of concern with no diagnosis attached.

## Verdict
`true` only if a specific diagnosis was explicitly labelled. The point of the item is the labelling, not the presence of the dangerous diagnosis.
