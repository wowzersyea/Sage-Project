# B6 — Something struck off, with the reason beside it

Decide whether a named diagnosis was ruled out **and** the feature arguing against it was stated.

## What counts
- "Transient synovitis is out — he's febrile at 38.6 with a CRP of 8.4 and he won't bear weight at all."
- Ruling out by illness script: "classic transient synovitis follows a viral illness and they usually still weight-bear; that is not this kid."

## What does not count
- Ruling out silently, or by omission from a later list.
- "I don't think it's that" with no feature named.
- Striking something that was never on the list.

## Verdict
`true` only if at least one named diagnosis was ruled out with a stated reason. Both halves are required.
