# A7 — Reveal named what was missed, and why

Decide whether, at the reveal, the presenter named something the team got wrong or came to late **and** gave a reason for it.

## What counts
- Naming a specific reasoning error: an item anchored on, a diagnosis reached for late, a discriminating test not asked for.
- Both halves are required: what was missed, and why it was missed.

## What does not count
- Stating the diagnosis and stopping.
- "Good discussion, you all got there" — praise is not a reveal.
- Naming what was missed with no account of why.

## Verdict
`true` only if both halves are present. Judge only the turns after the reveal begins.
