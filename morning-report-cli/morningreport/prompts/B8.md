# B8 — Confidence stated, and what would change my mind

Decide whether a working diagnosis was offered with **both** a confidence expression **and** an explicit falsifying condition.

## What counts as confidence
A percentage, or a calibrated phrase: "about 70%", "fairly sure", "leaning towards", "more likely than not". It must attach to a named working diagnosis.

## What counts as a trigger
An explicit statement of what would change their mind: "what would change my mind is a dry tap with a normal-looking joint — then I'm in bone, not joint."

## What does not count
- A diagnosis with neither. "I think it's septic arthritis" has no confidence and no trigger.
- Confidence with no trigger, or a trigger with no confidence. **Both halves are required.** If only one is present, the answer is `false`.
- Hedging that expresses no position: "it could be anything".

## Verdict
`true` only if both halves are present, attached to the same working diagnosis.
