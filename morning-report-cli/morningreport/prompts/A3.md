# A3 — One objective, read aloud at 0:00

Decide whether **exactly one** learning objective was stated aloud in the opening of the session.

## What counts
- A single objective, stated in the first 90 seconds, in the form of what the group should be able to distinguish, recognise, decide or do by the end.
- It may be read by the facilitator or the presenter.

## What does not count
- Naming the case, the topic or the chief complaint. "Today we have a toddler who won't walk" is a case, not an objective.
- Two or more objectives. The format allows one. Two is a fail, not a partial pass.
- An objective stated later, once the case is underway.

## Verdict
`true` only if exactly one objective was stated in the window. `false` if none, or if more than one.
