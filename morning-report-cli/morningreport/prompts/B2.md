# B2 — Three to four diagnoses, each with a reason

Count the **entity-level** diagnoses the PGY-1 offered in the first pass, and check that each carried a reason.

## What counts as an item
- A named entity that could be tested for and crossed off: "septic arthritis of the hip", "transient synovitis", "acute hepatitis A", "disseminated neonatal HSV".

## What does not count as an item
- A category or a framework branch: "infectious", "inflammatory", "oncologic", "serious bacterial infection". These belong on the framework line, not the list. Do not count them.
- A restatement of the data: "hepatitis" in a patient with transaminitis, "limp" in a child who will not walk.
- A sub-entity no management decision turns on: "HAV genotype IB".

## The reason
Each counted item needs an attached rationale — the feature that puts it on the list. "Septic arthritis, because of the positioning plus the CRP" has one; "septic arthritis" alone does not.

## Verdict
`true` only if **three or four** entity-level diagnoses were offered **and** every one carried a reason. Fewer than three, more than four, or any item without a reason: `false`.

Report the count you arrived at in your reasoning, and name any items you excluded and why.
