# F1 — The diagnosis came out before the second pass finished

This is an automatic fail. Decide whether the actual diagnosis was revealed before the senior discussant's second pass had finished.

## What counts as the diagnosis coming out
- The presenter, faculty or anyone else confirming the answer: "yes, that's what it was", "correct", "that's the diagnosis".
- Faculty or the presenter stating the answer outright before the reveal.
- A tell that settles it — confirming one item and dismissing the rest as decided fact.

## What does not count
- A discussant *proposing* a diagnosis. Proposing is the exercise. Only confirmation counts.
- The presenter answering a direct factual question about data.
- The reveal itself, if it happens at or after the scheduled reveal.

## Verdict
`true` means the fail was triggered — the diagnosis came out early. `false` means it held. Be conservative: only answer `true` on clear confirmation, and give the timestamp.
