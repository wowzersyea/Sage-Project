# A4 — Hook first, not demographics

Decide whether the presenter's first substantive turn opened on the clinical situation rather than a recitation of background.

## What counts
- Opening at the decisive moment: the presentation, the duration, and one provocative datum — the vital sign, the exam finding, the image that made the case worth 25 minutes.
- A one-line framing that leaves the group wanting the next fact.

## What does not count
- Opening with past medical history, medication lists, family history, birth history or social history.
- A chronological narrative that begins well before the presenting problem.
- Naming the diagnosis or the answer.

## Verdict
`true` if the first substantive presenter turn led with the clinical situation. `false` if it led with background.
