Identifiers

What is written here is read by other people. These have to be dealt with before it can be sent.

Anonymous, and it stays that way Nothing here asks who you are and there is no account. The one thing asked about you is which of the four groups you are in, because an intern and a faculty member are describing different experiences of the same mornings and averaging them together would lose both.

That does have a limit, and it is worth saying out loud rather than burying: in a program this size a group of one or two is not anonymous. The read-out therefore never breaks out a group with fewer than three responses — those answers are counted in the total and nowhere else. Written answers are shown without a group label attached, and out of the order they arrived in.

This is a measurement of an arrangement, taken before that arrangement changes. It is not an evaluation of any person, it never becomes one, and no view or export here joins anything you write to a name.
