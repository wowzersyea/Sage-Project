Sage Project · Pediatric morning report

Morning Report

Twenty-five minutes, one case, two sites. Six tools over one shared data folder, so that finishing a session feeds the next thing that needs the data — and nobody retypes what they already typed.

Baseline Answered once, before any of this changes.

Pre-intervention evaluation

What the current arrangement is like, rated by the people in it: the call across the two sites, how much a morning varies from the last one, who actually speaks, what was left at the end of it, and what three mornings a week cost. One tap says PGY-1, PGY-2, PGY-3 or faculty; nothing else is asked about the respondent. Six or seven minutes, anonymous, and it answers to sageproject.xyz/baseline.

Baseline QR code

The code and the short address to hand round — on a slide, taped to the workroom door, or sent as a link. Printable, and downloadable as SVG or PNG.

Baseline read-out

Domain scores by year group, the counted quantities, and both open questions — each row tagged outcome, process or balancing, so the cost of a change sits on the same table as the good it did. Nothing is modelled and nothing is drafted: reversed items are turned round, groups of fewer than three are not broken out, and the arithmetic is the same arithmetic you could do in a spreadsheet — which the CSV button hands you.
Before The week ahead, and the 24 hours before report.

Draw discussants

Two wheels, weighted so whoever has gone longest without the role comes up first. Drawn names stay struck off across both sites.

Roster and equity

Who has served, who has not, and who is away. Sorted by weeks since a discussant role — the top row is who you draw next.

Role cards

Seven cards, one URL each, so the facilitator can send a resident exactly their card with the one-liner.
During Screen-shared from the scribe's seat.

Board

The zoned board with the 25-minute clock, forced rationale and the two lab blocks. Autosaves every 20 seconds.
After The board hands these over when it finishes.

Scorecard

The sixteen-item strike-out card. What the board already knows is ticked for you, marked, and overridable.

Capture case

The case bank entry, pre-filled from the board and checked for identifiers. Under sixty seconds or it will not happen.

Feedback

Ninety seconds, anonymous: the technology, the flow, the six roles, and the one thing to change. Say it into the microphone if typing is what stops you. Works from a phone, and answers to the short address sageproject.xyz/feedback.

Feedback summary

What came back, rolled up and — if you set a key — drafted into a summary of each section and each role, one call at a time.
Later Weeks and months out.

Review game

Four rounds off the case bank, filtered by date and tag. Round three is backwards and is the one worth keeping.

Group report

Which rubric item the group misses most, over a rolling window. Never a single session, never a person.

Learn

How specific a differential item has to be before it earns a place on the board. Three cases to sort.

Handouts

The printed collateral: role cards, the bias card, the role evaluation forms.

Setting up the data folder

There is no server and no account. The tools read and write JSON files in a folder you choose. Point that folder at a OneDrive-synced location and both sites share state with nothing in between.

  1. Make a folder called MorningReport somewhere OneDrive syncs.
  2. Press Connect data folder in the bar at the top and pick it. Grant read-write.
  3. Do the same once on the other site's machine, pointing at the same synced folder.
MorningReport/
  roster.json          names and the participation log — the one file with names in it
  casebank/            de-identified cases, permanent
  baseline/            the pre-intervention evaluation, permanent — the before picture
  sessions/            de-identified scorecards, permanent
    feedback/          what the group said about the session, permanent
  board-archive/       de-identified board contents, permanent
  working/             identified, ephemeral, CLI and feedback only, swept after 7 days
    feedback/          the role half of the feedback — handed over, then gone
      audio/           recordings, only the ones somebody chose to keep

Folder access needs Chrome or Edge. In any other browser the tools fall back to explicit Download JSON and Load JSON buttons, and the bar says so.

Optional: a shared roster

The folder is per-machine. If you would rather have one live answer to who is on service today — editable by a chief from a phone, picked up by every browser without anyone re-exporting anything — the roster and rota can come from a Google Sheet behind a keyed endpoint instead.

  1. Settings — paste the endpoint URL and key, once per browser.
  2. Publish — seed the sheet from the folder you already have.
  3. After that, a chief edits the sheet. Swaps and sick calls need no export.

Settings also draws the QR code for the presenter template: scan it and a phone can send feedback without anyone typing a URL, and the board puts the same code on screen at the end of a session.

Entirely optional, and it degrades rather than misleads: with no endpoint, or one that cannot be reached, everything falls back to the folder exactly as before. The folder always wins over the endpoint, and the participation log never leaves it.

What this module will not do Everything in casebank/, sessions/ and board-archive/ is permanent and de-identified. roster.json records that a resident served a role on a date, for scheduling. Nothing here records how a named person performed, and no view, export or query joins a name to a score. Nothing here is summative, and nothing may be exported as a milestone or evaluation record.

One thing is deliberately not anonymous, and only if somebody chooses it: a feedback recording, because a voice identifies its speaker. Those are never automatic, they live in working/ with the role notes, and they sweep on the same seven-day clock. They are formative like the rest — not a record of anybody, and never an input to one.
