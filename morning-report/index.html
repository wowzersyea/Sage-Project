Sage Project · Pediatric morning report

Morning Report

Twenty-five minutes, one case, two sites. Two people carry it — the intern at 0:07 and the senior at 0:11 — and everything on this page exists to get those two ready and put their thinking on a board.

Roles Read your card before report. It is one page and it takes four minutes.

PGY-1 discussant 0:07

The first pass. Four deliverables in order: problem representation, three to four diagnoses with the reason each is on the list, the can't-miss named as such, and the one piece of data you most want. A defensible four beats an unfalsifiable twelve. Nobody interrupts you.

Senior discussant 0:11

The second pass. You are not producing a better list — you are turning the intern's list into an argument. Five moves: organise into a framework, prune out loud with the reason, apply the illness script, name the discriminating test, state confidence, trigger and bias.

Frameworks

The eight the senior can offer at 0:11 — anatomic by level or by layer, physiologic, pre/intra/post, mechanism, age-anchored, tempo, triage — each with when it is the right one and what it sounds like out loud. Named before the list, or it is a label rather than a tool.

Cognitive bias card

Fourteen biases, each with the one question that interrupts it. The senior's fifth move is to name the bias they are watching for in themselves. Print it and keep it by the board.

All seven role cards

Presenter, scribe, faculty, facilitator and the run of show as well — one URL each, so the facilitator can send a resident exactly their card with the one-liner.
Before The 24 hours before report.

Draw discussants

Two wheels, weighted so whoever has gone longest without the role comes up first. Drawn names stay struck off across both sites.

Presenter slide template

Eight slides with the two pauses built in. Type over the brackets, keep history ahead of labs, delete the how-to slide. No PHI, ever.
During Screen-shared from the scribe's seat.

Board

The zoned board with the 25-minute clock, forced rationale and the two lab blocks. Autosaves every 20 seconds.

Parked tools — the baseline evaluation, the roster, the scorecard, capture, feedback and the review game. All still working, all still pointed at the same folder, just off this page until the twenty-five minutes are running the way they should.

Administration — setting up the shared data folder, and the optional shared roster. Done once per machine; nobody presenting or discussing a case needs it.

What this module will not do Everything in casebank/, sessions/ and board-archive/ is permanent and de-identified. roster.json records that a resident served a role on a date, for scheduling. Nothing here records how a named person performed, and no view, export or query joins a name to a score. Nothing here is summative, and nothing may be exported as a milestone or evaluation record.

One thing is deliberately not anonymous, and only if somebody chooses it: a feedback recording, because a voice identifies its speaker. Those are never automatic, they live in working/ with the role notes, and they sweep on the same seven-day clock. They are formative like the rest — not a record of anybody, and never an input to one.
