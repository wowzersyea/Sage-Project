Morning report · before

Role cards

Seven cards, one URL each. The facilitator sends the one-liner 24 hours ahead anyway — attaching a link to exactly that person's card, and nothing else, is what makes the card get read. A seven-page PDF attachment does not get read.

Handouts

The printed collateral, as it is. Only the role cards get the single-source treatment — they are the ones that get emailed and the ones whose content changes.

Printing

Every card page carries print CSS: Print → Save as PDF reproduces the printed handout on one page, white ground, same layout. There is no separate PDF pipeline to keep in step — change a timing in content/roles.json and the web page, the print view and the run-of-show table all follow.
