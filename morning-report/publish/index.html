    This sends names to your endpoint That is the point of it, and it is the only thing here that sends names anywhere. It goes to the Apps Script URL you configured and nowhere else — not to this site, not into the repository. Publishing replaces the Roster, Rota and Sites tabs; anything a chief has edited there since the last publish is overwritten. It does not touch Draws or Feedback — what the room has recorded is safe from a re-publish.
