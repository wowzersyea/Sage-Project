Anonymous, unless you keep a recording Nothing here asks who you are, and there is no account. What you write about the session — the technology, the flow, the overall — is kept with the session and belongs to nobody in particular. What you write about a role goes to working/, which is identified by inference and is swept after seven days: the roster records who held which role on a date, so a permanent role file would join a name to a judgement. Role feedback is formative, it is handed to the person, and it is never filed or reported.

A recording is a different matter, and the one thing on this page that is not anonymous: everybody here knows everybody's voice. Recordings are discarded when you press send unless you tick the box that keeps them, and a kept one goes to working/ and is swept on the same clock. The identifier check reads the words, so it can stop you sending a written MRN — it cannot take one out of the audio.
