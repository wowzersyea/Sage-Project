Privacy Policy — Immunopolis: Sage Defense

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Immunopolis: Sage Defense ("the game") is part of the Sage Project. This policy describes what information the game handles and how.

Data stored on your device

Game progress — unlocked chapters, cleared levels, token balance, daily bonus timing, and sound preference — is stored only in your browser's (or app's) local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and is deleted if you clear the site's data or use the in-game "Reset Progress" option.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as page views and approximate region) that help us understand how the game is used. Google Analytics may set cookies for this purpose. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes this data.

What we do not collect

  • No account, registration, or sign-in of any kind
  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No purchases or payment information — all in-game tokens are earned by playing
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties

Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no ads, and no purchases, and collects no personal information from any user, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the Sage Project issue tracker.
