## Full description (max 4000 chars — ~1900 used)

Pathogens have breached the bloodstream — and you command the immune system.

Immunopolis: Sage Defense is a strategy tower-defense game set inside the
human body. Deploy immune cells and antimicrobial batteries along the
vessels, upgrade them down branching paths, and hold the line against
bacteria, viruses, fungi, and pan-resistant superbugs.

FIVE-CHAPTER STORY CAMPAIGN
Fight from the first breach in the bloodstream, through the lymph nodes and
deep tissue, into the bone marrow, and make your last stand at the heart —
with hand-crafted maps, escalating enemy pools, and boss finales, including
a twin-superbug last stand.

ENDLESS MODE: SEPTIC SURGE
Face an open-ended surge that grows stronger every wave, with a superbug
arriving every tenth wave. No victory — only the record of how long you
held. Your personal best is saved.

FIVE TOWERS, TEN UPGRADE PATHS
Macrophages engulf single targets. Neutrophils lob splash bursts (but can't
touch flying viruses). Antibiotic Batteries melt armor with stacking toxins.
T-Cell Snipers strike from across the map. Fever Coils freeze whole
corridors. Every tower has two upgrade paths and you can't max both — every
placement is a build decision.

ENEMIES THAT MAKE YOU ADAPT
Fast strep chains, armored MRSA clusters, flying viruses that slip past
ground defenses, tanky fungal spores, and biofilm fortresses. Armor,
flight, and pack tactics mean no single tower type can hold alone.

EARN, DON'T PAY
Every token is earned by playing — clear chapters, defeat bosses, and claim
your daily booster. No ads, no purchases, no account. Progress saves on
your device, and the whole game plays offline.

STRATEGY IN EVERY DETAIL
Deterministic waves you can learn and master. Hero abilities on cooldown.
1×/2×/3× game speed. Sell and rebuild. Designed for touch.

The Sage Project · No data collected · Plays offline
